# Shopping list: Roland's home OT kit

Prices are rough estimates from Takealot and South African shops, researched on 5 October 2026. They will change.

Enough for every activity on the weekly plan.

## Where to shop

- [Takealot](https://www.takealot.com): Most items on the list.
- [Tiny Tree Toys](https://tinytreetoys.co.za): OT-owned SA shop: scooter boards, balance and fine-motor toys.
- [HiTech Therapy](https://hitechtherapyonline.co.za): Theraputty and therapy putty.
- [Playing and Learning](https://www.playingandlearning.co.za/collections/occupational-therapy): OT collection, Johannesburg.
- [SA Toy Trade](https://satoytrade.co.za): Therapy and teaching aids.

**Left-handed scissors:** check the blades are reversed (the top blade is on the left when he holds them). "Ambidextrous" scissors usually have right-handed blades.
